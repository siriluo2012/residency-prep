Step 1 tests whether you can apply foundational science to clinical vignettes. Since January 2022 it is reported pass/fail, which shifts the goal from maximizing a score to passing with confidence and preserving energy for Step 2 CK.

<ExamFacts exam="usmle-step1" />

## Blueprint

The official specification weights systems as ranges. Our question bank and dashboard use these exact ranges so your attempt distribution can be compared to the blueprint.

<Blueprint exam="usmle-step1" />

Disciplines cut across systems: pathology 45-55%, physiology 30-40%, and then pharmacology, microbiology, gross anatomy and embryology each 10-20%, with biochemistry, immunology, histology, genetics, nutrition and behavioral sciences in the 5-20% band.
