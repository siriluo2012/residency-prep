ECFMG Certification is the credential that lets a graduate of a medical school outside the United States and Canada enter ACGME-accredited residency training and sit for USMLE Step 3. The requirements have four parts; you can work on them in parallel, but the Certificate is only issued when all four are satisfied.

## Pathways 1-6 in one table

ECFMG retired the Step 2 Clinical Skills exam in 2021. The Pathways are the replacement for demonstrating clinical and communication skills. You apply for exactly one.

Pathway 6 is the catch-all; evaluators must meet ECFMG's criteria and submit through ECFMG's system. Check eligibility for Pathways 3 and 4 first, because they require no new assessment if your school qualifies.

## English proficiency: why OET Medicine

ECFMG accepts OET Medicine (test center or OET@Home) as the only English proficiency test for Certification. You need grade B in Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking in a single test administration. Our [OET Medicine module](/oet) covers all four sub-tests, including Claude-graded referral letters against the official writing criteria.

## Common mistakes

- Applying for a Pathway before confirming eligibility, then having the application rejected after the deadline.
- Letting the medical school's verification stall. Follow up with the registrar directly.
- Taking OET before the Writing sub-test is ready. A single B-grade miss means re-sitting all four sub-tests.
- Assuming Step 2 CK is optional for the Match. Most programs filter on it.
