OET is an English test built around healthcare workplace tasks: reading a hospital policy, listening to a consultation, writing a referral letter, and talking to a patient. For ECFMG you need grade B (350 of 500) in each sub-test from one sitting.

## Where physicians lose marks

- **Writing: content selection.** The case notes contain more than you need. Grade B letters select what the recipient must know and omit the rest. Our tasks include the reviewer's list of key content points so you can self-check, and the Claude grader scores each official criterion separately.
- **Writing: note form.** Expand abbreviations and write complete sentences. "Pt c/o SOB" is a fail.
- **Speaking: jargon.** Explain conditions in lay terms and check understanding.
- **Reading Part A: time.** Fifteen minutes for 20 items means you cannot read the texts fully; practice scanning for the item's keyword.
