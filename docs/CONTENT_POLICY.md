# Content Policy

This document is binding for every item (question, flashcard, animation, OET task, guide) that enters the system. The pipeline enforces the machine-checkable parts; reviewers enforce the rest.

## 1. Allowed sources

| Source | License | What we use | Attribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| MedQA (Jin et al. 2020), HF `awinml/medqa` | MIT | USMLE-style 4-option items as *seed/reference* material and for dedupe. Items may be served after physician review and rewriting of explanations. | "MedQA, Jin et al. 2020 (MIT)" |
| Cohort / MD3 open corpus (`todd866/cohort`, `open-content/usmle/step1`) | CC BY 4.0 (items), MIT (figures manifest) | Original Step 1 questions and SVG diagrams. Served after review. | "MD3 contributors, CC BY 4.0, https://github.com/todd866/cohort" |
| Claude-generated originals | Original work, owned by this project | Vignettes, explanations, flashcards, animation components, OET tasks | none required; `source=claude` |
| Hand-authored by reviewers | Original | Any | `source=human` |
| USMLE, ECFMG, OET official sites | Public facts | Blueprint percentages, fees, eligibility, deadlines, retake rules. Facts only, paraphrased, with URL and `verified_at` date. | Link to official page |
| open-exam-prep.com and similar | All rights reserved | **Structure only**: topic taxonomy, chapter ordering, which concepts are grouped together. **Never question text, explanations, or prose.** | none (structure is not copyrightable; we still cite as inspiration in internal notes) |

Anything not in this table is disallowed until added here with a license review. In particular: NBME/USMLE sample items, UWorld, AMBOSS, First Aid, Pathoma, Sketchy, AnKing or other Anki decks, textbook scans, recalled exam questions.

## 2. Originality rule

- Every question must be an **original vignette**, not a recalled exam item and not a paraphrase of a copyrighted item.
- `pipeline/qa/dedupe.py` embeds each new item and rejects anything with cosine similarity >= 0.92 to any MedQA, Cohort, or crawled reference text (configurable). Items in 0.85-0.92 are flagged `needs_originality_check` for the reviewer.
- Generation prompts forbid naming or imitating commercial QBanks.

## 3. License tracking

Each item carries `source`, `source_ref`, `license`, `attribution`. The web app refuses to publish an item whose `license` is not in the allow-list in `packages/schema/schemas/common.json` (`LicenseId`). This is a fail-closed boundary: missing license means not publishable.

## 4. Review gate

- Items are created with `status=draft`, pass auto-QA to `in_review`, and need a reviewer decision before `approved`. Publishing (`published`) is a separate explicit action by an admin/reviewer and sets `published_at`.
- Required approvals: questions 1 physician; flashcards 1 reviewer (physician or senior student); animations 1 physician (accuracy) plus a render test pass; OET writing/speaking tasks 1 reviewer with OET familiarity.
- Reviewers can edit in place; the edited payload is stored in `review_votes.edited_payload` and the diff is kept.
- A rejection must include a reason; rejections are exported as negative few-shot examples for the generator.

## 5. Clinical safety

- Explanations must not give patient-specific medical advice; they explain exam reasoning.
- Every published item has a "Report an error" control that creates an `item_feedback` row and, after 2 reports, automatically moves the item back to `in_review`.
- Dosing, thresholds, and guideline statements must cite the year/guideline in the explanation so reviewers can verify.

## 6. Crawler conduct

- Honor `robots.txt`; identify as `ResidencyPrepBot/0.1 (+contact URL)`.
- Max 1 request/second per host, cache responses locally, never re-crawl within 24 h.
- Store only extracted structure/facts plus the URL and fetch timestamp; discard page HTML after extraction except in the local cache.

## 7. AI disclosure

Published items generated by Claude are labeled "AI-drafted, physician-reviewed by <initials>, <date>" in the UI.
